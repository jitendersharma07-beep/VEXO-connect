# ATC POS — cashier guide

Print this and keep it by the till. One page, in order of what you actually do.

**Open the till:** `https://atcworkspace.com/pos` → sign in with the email and
password your manager gave you. The first time, the system will make you choose
a new password. Nobody at ATC can see it.

---

## Taking an order

1. **Sell** in the left menu.
2. Pick the branch (if you work at more than one), then **Dine-in** and a table,
   or **Takeaway**.
   - Tapping a table that is already occupied **resumes** that table's order. It
     does not start a second one.
3. Tap products to add them. Search the top box if the menu is long.
   - Items with sizes ask you which one before they go on the bill.
4. Change a quantity with **+ / −** on the line.

## Sending food to the kitchen

Press **Send KOT**. The kitchen ticket prints: item names and quantities, no
prices.

**Once a line is sent to the kitchen its quantity is locked.** That is on
purpose — the kitchen is already cooking it. If the customer changes their mind,
ask a manager to void the line.

You can send KOT more than once on the same order. Each press sends only what is
new since last time.

## Discounts

**Discount** applies to the whole bill — a flat rupee amount or a percentage.
Individual lines can be discounted from the line's edit control.

## Billing

Press **Bill**. The order gets its invoice number and is locked for editing.
Nothing can be added after this.

If you pressed **Bill** by mistake and the customer wants to add something,
start a new order for the extra items. Do not ask a manager to void the bill
just to add a coffee.

## Taking the money

Press the payment button and choose **Cash**, **Card**, **UPI** or **Other**.

- **Cash** — type what the customer handed you in *Cash tendered*. The system
  works out the change. Do not type the bill amount when they gave you more.
- **Card / UPI / Other** — type the amount received. Use the note box for
  anything useful later, e.g. `GPay ref 4417`.

Then **Record payment**.

> **Everything you record here is you saying the money arrived.** The system did
> not check with the bank. If a UPI payment fails on the customer's phone after
> you have recorded it, tell your manager immediately — the bill will say paid
> when it isn't.

Split payments are fine: record each one. The balance due updates as you go.

**If the screen freezes or you are not sure a payment saved, do not press it
again.** Reload the page and look at the order. The system refuses a duplicate
payment, but checking is faster than explaining.

## Printing

**Receipt** opens the customer bill; press **Print**. Reprint the same way from
**Orders** at any time.

---

## What you cannot do, and who can

| | |
|---|---|
| Void a line already sent to the kitchen | Manager or owner |
| Void a whole order | Manager or owner |
| Refund money | Manager or owner |
| Change the menu or prices | Owner |
| File the daily closing | Manager or owner |

This is not a lack of trust; it is so the day's figures have one person's name
against them.

## End of your shift

Tell your manager the drawer is ready to count. Do not count it into the system
yourself — **Daily closing** is the manager's screen, and the count needs to be
done by someone who did not take the money.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Licence expired" or you cannot bill** — call the owner. ATC support can
  reinstate it in a minute; you cannot.
- **The page will not load** — check another site works. If only POS is down,
  call ATC support.
- **You wrote down a sale on paper because the system was down** — keep the
  paper. Enter it when the system is back and tell your manager, so the drawer
  count matches.
